outbound-autosend-gate

A go-live gate for an AI outbound sales agent, and a ladder that decides which sequence steps may send without a rep. 30 constructed accounts with usage signals, research facts and CRM traps, 12 rule checks and 2 Claude judge checks, built on golive-gate.

The recorded live run of the Claude agent is being added. Until then, see the no-model mail-merge baseline blocked by the same gate: it emails opted-out contacts and existing customers, names users, fakes a reply subject and offers a discount.
